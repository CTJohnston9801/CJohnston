About

A bit about me

I'm a hydrogeologist at SLR, where I assess and help protect groundwater resources for clients across the water, environmental, and resources sectors. My work spans groundwater investigations, numerical modelling, and managing the impacts of development on water.

I enjoy turning complex subsurface data into clear, defensible advice that helps clients make sound decisions and meet their environmental obligations. I bring a practical, evidence-led approach to every project.

  • Groundwater AssessmentCharacterising aquifers and groundwater systems.
  • Numerical ModellingSimulating groundwater flow and contaminant transport.
  • Contaminated LandInvestigating and remediating impacted sites.
  • Water ResourcesSupporting sustainable supply and licensing.
