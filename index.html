About

A bit about me

I'm a Senior Hydrogeologist with SLR Consulting (Canada) Ltd., and have worked on groundwater supply development, planning, and protection projects for over 30 years. I've led key projects for many Ontario municipalities — including Groundwater Under the Direct Influence of Surface Water (GUDI) assessments — and worked with the Ministry to review and update technical guidance.

My municipal water supply experience includes developing large-capacity bedrock and overburden well fields, securing well field and regional Permits To Take Water, and developing protection plans for point- and non-point-source contaminants including nitrate, 1,4-dioxane, TCE, and road salts. Whether providing strategic advice, technical direction, or project management, I bring a common-sense approach and a dedication to quality.

  • Municipal Water SupplyDeveloping bedrock & overburden well fields and securing PTTWs.
  • Source Water ProtectionWellhead protection, GUDI, and microbial control plans.
  • Contaminant HydrogeologyNitrate, 1,4-dioxane, TCE, and road-salt assessment.
